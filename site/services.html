What I do

New installations

A complete 3CX build: extensions, users, phones, and a configuration that matches how your business actually takes calls.

Migrations

SIP trunks

Call flows

Hosting & upgrades

Deploy 3CX on hosted, cloud, or your own server, and keep it current with version upgrades and backups.

Ongoing support

Troubleshooting call quality, registration, and routing problems, plus changes as your team grows.

Automated deployments

Instead of building each 3CX server by hand, I can define it in code with Terraform and Ansible. Every deployment then comes out the same, on whichever VPS or cloud platform you prefer.

Terraform

Creates the server, networking, firewall rules, and DNS on your chosen platform, such as AWS, DigitalOcean, Linode, Vultr, or another VPS provider.

Ansible

Prepares the operating system and installs and configures 3CX, along with updates, hardening, and backups, the same way every time.

  • Every deployment is identical, so there are fewer surprises and problems are faster to track down.
  • New sites or replacement servers go up quickly, from a setup that's already been tested.
  • Your setup is documented as code, not stored in one person's head.
  • Moving to a different hosting provider later is much simpler.

Questions

Do you need to be on site?

No. Everything is done remotely. If you have desk phones, I'll provide setup steps your team can follow, or work with your local IT.

Can I keep my existing phone numbers?

In most cases, yes. Numbers can usually be ported to your new SIP provider, and I'll plan the cutover around the port date.

Will we have downtime during the switch?

The new system is built and tested alongside your old one, so the cutover itself is usually short and can be scheduled outside business hours.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the system and what's involved. Send me a few details and I'll give you a quote.
